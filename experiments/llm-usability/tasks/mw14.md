# Task: implement the MW14 weekly momentum book

MW14 is a long-only weekly momentum book on S&P 1500 stocks. Implement it as
an abt strategy named `mw14` that reproduces the book's rules exactly as
specified below.

## Data

Bundle: `~/data/abt/b_mw14` (environment `equities_1w`, see
`env/equities_1w.dsl`). One bar per market week, keyed at the week's last
trading day, 1990-01 to 2026-07. Per stock and week:

- the stock's last raw (unadjusted) close of the week;
- a running total-return index (dividends and splits folded in): this is the
  price the book trades and marks at;
- the week's split factor, present only in weeks with a split (a split-adjusted
  close is the raw close times the cumulative product of the split factors
  over the stock's own weeks so far);
- the week's mean daily dollar volume;
- whether the stock is in the trading universe that week, and whether it is a
  member of the S&P 1500 (the label `SP1500`, the union of the S&P 500, 400
  and 600) at its week end;
- a delisting event the week after a stock stops trading.

Market series by name, one value a week: `SPXTR` (the S&P 500 total-return
index), `VIX`, and `VIX_MA4` (the VIX's own 4-week mean, precomputed).

A stock's history is "its own rows": the weeks in which it is in the universe.
All rolling windows below count a stock's own rows (not calendar weeks), and
need the full window length of rows to produce a value.

## Rules (evaluated every week, at the week's close)

1. **Universe (eligible).** In the S&P 1500 that week, raw close >= $3, and
   the mean over the stock's last 4 rows of the weekly dollar volume is at
   least $2,000,000.
2. **Score (Clenow momentum).** Over the stock's last 18 rows, fit an OLS
   regression of the natural log of the split-adjusted close on the row number
   (0, 1, 2, ... counting the stock's rows since its first). With slope b and
   correlation R of that fit, score = (exp(52 b) - 1) x R². No 18 rows, no
   score.
3. **Rank.** Rank eligible stocks with a score by score, best first, rank 1 =
   best; ties broken by asset id ascending (ordinal ranks, no shared ranks).
4. **Gate.** The SPXTR value is above its own mean over the last 52 weeks
   (current week included).
5. **Liquidity decile.** Rank the eligible stocks by their 4-row mean dollar
   volume ascending, ties sharing the average rank; with n eligible stocks, the
   stock is liquid when rank / n x 10 > 4 (i.e. in the top six deciles).
6. **Enter.** rank <= 7, the gate holds, and the stock is liquid.
7. **Stay.** rank <= 28, or the split-adjusted close is above its mean over
   the stock's last 40 rows.
8. **Latch (in the book).** A stock is latched this week when it may stay and
   either it enters this week or it was latched at its own previous row. A
   stock that may not stay is not latched. (A stock's first row latches only
   if it enters.)
9. **Panic.** If neither the gate holds nor VIX is at or below VIX_MA4, the
   book's scale is 0 (no names held). A week with no VIX reading counts as not
   calm.
10. **Crowding dial.** Among latched stocks in the universe that have a 40-row
    mean, if more than 25% have a split-adjusted close more than 50% above
    their 40-row mean, the scale is 0.70; otherwise 1.
11. **Weights.** The book holds the latched stocks that are eligible this
    week. With N such stocks, each gets weight min(scale / N, 0.15), kept only
    when above 0.000001.
12. **Trading band.** Trades happen at the week's close. A held stock's
    current weight is its position (shares x total-return index price) over
    the fixed $1,000,000 base. A new target is traded only when it differs
    from the current weight by at least 2.5% (with a 1e-12 tolerance:
    difference >= 0.025 - 1e-12); a new name is bought only when its target is
    at least 2.5%. A held stock that is no longer a target is always sold.

## Run conventions

- Fixed $1,000,000 base, no compounding; fractional shares.
- Trade at the decision week's close, at the total-return index price
  (dividends and splits are already in that price).
- Costs: 10 basis points of traded notional on each side, nothing else (no
  per-share commission, no slippage, no financing).
- Leverage is never a constraint; a delisted holding is closed at its last
  price.
- Report weekly returns (52 periods a year) from 1991-01-01; save the weekly
  returns with `--returns`.
- The bundle's data-quality test flags small-cap weekly moves; run it with
  `--untested`.
- A full run takes about 2 minutes; for iteration run a short window such as
  `--start 1990-01-01 --end 1995-12-31` (about 15 s).
